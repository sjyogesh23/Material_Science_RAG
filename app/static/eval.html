How these are computed
          

            
Per class
Precision = correct predictions of the class / all predictions of it; recall = correct / all gold items of it; F1 = their harmonic mean; support = gold items of the class. Both classes of each matrix are scored (the negative class uses TN as its hits).

            
Micro
Pooled over both classes: total correct / total items. For a single-label yes/no decision micro precision = recall = F1 = accuracy.

            
Macro
Unweighted mean over the classes that occur in the gold labels, so the rare class (e.g. 14 out-of-context items of 90) counts as much as the common one. A class that is never predicted counts as precision 0.

            
Weighted
Mean over classes weighted by support.

          

        

      


      

        

          
Micro vs macro averages

          
Micro = mean over items (big scenarios weigh more). Macro = mean of the per-scenario means (each scenario counts once).
